# ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Compliance Monitoring: A Practical Guide

Passing an ISO 27001 or SOC 2 audit isn't a one-time event — it's a state you have to *maintain*. Auditors don't just want policies on paper; they want **evidence** that controls are working continuously.

That evidence comes from **monitoring**.

---

## Why Monitoring Is Central to Compliance

Both frameworks are built on the idea that you can demonstrate control over your information systems. In practice that means you can answer:

- Who has access to sensitive data?
- What did they do with it, and when?
- How would you detect and respond to a security event?
- Can you prove all of the above with logs and audit trails?

Without continuous monitoring, every audit becomes a frantic scramble for evidence.

---

## ISO 27001: Controls That Rely on Monitoring

ISO 27001 (Annex A) includes controls that effectively require activity logging and oversight, such as:

- **Access control** — track who accesses what.
- **Logging and monitoring** — record events and review them.
- **Operations security** — detect malware and anomalies.
- **Removable media controls** — govern and log USB usage.

A monitoring tool that logs access and device activity provides direct evidence for these.

---

## SOC 2: The Trust Services Criteria

SOC 2 evaluates controls against criteria like **Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy**. Auditors look for:

- **Continuous monitoring** of systems and access.
- **Audit trails** showing who did what.
- **Incident detection and response** evidence.
- **Change and access reviews** over the audit period.

For a **Type II** report especially, you must show controls operated *consistently over time* — which is impossible without ongoing monitoring.

---

## Staying Audit-Ready Year-Round

1. **Log access and activity** continuously across endpoints.
2. **Retain audit trails** for the full audit period.
3. **Monitor sensitive data movement** (file and USB activity).
4. **Detect and document incidents** as they happen.
5. **Review access regularly** and record the reviews.
6. **Keep evidence centralised** so audit prep takes hours, not weeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does monitoring software make me ISO 27001 or SOC 2 compliant?**
No tool alone makes you compliant — but continuous monitoring provides essential evidence for many required controls and makes audits far smoother.

**What's the difference between ISO 27001 and SOC 2?**
ISO 27001 is an international standard you get *certified* against; SOC 2 is an *attestation report* (common in North America). Both rely heavily on monitoring and audit trails.

**How long must I keep audit logs?**
Through the audit period and your retention policy — commonly a year for SOC 2 Type II.
