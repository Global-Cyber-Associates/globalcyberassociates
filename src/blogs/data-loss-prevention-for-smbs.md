# Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Explained for Startups and SMBs

Your business runs on data — customer records, financial details, source code, contracts. **Data Loss Prevention (DLP)** is how you stop that data from leaving without permission.

Many founders assume DLP is only for large enterprises. It isn't. Startups and SMBs are *frequent* targets precisely because attackers expect weaker controls.

---

## What Is Data Loss Prevention?

DLP is a set of practices and tools designed to **detect, monitor, and prevent the unauthorised movement of sensitive data** — whether that's an accidental leak or deliberate theft.

Good DLP answers three questions at all times:

1. **Where** is our sensitive data?
2. **Who** is accessing or moving it?
3. **How** is it leaving — and should it be?

---

## How Data Actually Leaks

Most data loss isn't a Hollywood-style hack. It's mundane:

- 📁 Copying files to a **USB drive** or personal hard disk
- ☁️ Uploading documents to **personal cloud storage** (Drive, Dropbox)
- 📧 **Emailing** sensitive files to the wrong recipient — or to a personal address
- 💬 Sharing data through chat apps and unmanaged tools
- 👋 **Departing employees** taking files on their way out

Notice the pattern: most of these are *file movements by insiders*. That's why file and USB activity monitoring is the backbone of practical DLP.

---

## DLP for SMBs: A Practical, Affordable Approach

You don't need a million-dollar enterprise suite. Start here:

### 1. Classify Your Data
Identify what's truly sensitive — customer PII, payment data, IP, credentials. You can't protect what you haven't defined.

### 2. Monitor File & Device Activity
Log every copy, transfer, and USB connection involving sensitive files. Visibility alone deters and detects most leaks.

### 3. Control Removable Media
USB drives are the #1 quiet exit route for data. Track every device that connects.

### 4. Keep Audit Trails
When something does happen, you need to know *who, what, when, and where* — for investigation and compliance (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, India's DPDP Act).

### 5. Train Your People
Most accidental leaks are preventable with simple awareness. (See our [cybersecurity tips for everyone](https://www.globalcyberassociates.com/blog/cybersecurity-tips).)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between DLP and antivirus?**
Antivirus stops *malware*. DLP stops *data* from leaving. You need both.

**Do small businesses really need DLP?**
Yes — SMBs hold valuable data and are often targeted for having lighter defences. Affordable, monitoring-based DLP closes the most common gaps.

**Does DLP block my employees from working?**
