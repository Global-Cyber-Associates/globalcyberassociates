# VisuN AI for BPO: Turning Workforce Visibility into Productivity, Accountability & Data Protection

The Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) industry is built around people, processes, technology and customer trust.

Every day, BPO employees handle customer information, financial records, healthcare information, credentials, business documents and confidential client data. At the same time, BPO organizations are expected to deliver higher productivity, faster turnaround times, better quality and stronger data protection — often across thousands of employees and multiple shifts.

This creates a critical question: **how can a BPO company know whether its workforce is productive, accountable and handling customer data responsibly?** This is where VisuN AI can play an important role.

---

## 📊 The BPO Industry Is Massive — and Highly Dependent on People

India's BPM industry reached approximately $49 billion in FY2024, with exports representing the dominant portion of the market. NASSCOM also reports that BPM organizations are increasing technology investment and adopting GenAI across functions such as customer service and finance & accounting.

The broader Indian IT-BPM industry had approximately 5.8 million employees in FY2024-25, according to NASSCOM. MeitY, citing NASSCOM, estimates the Indian IT-ITeS industry at around 6 million professionals in FY2025-26. That means the scale of the workforce — and therefore the scale of productivity and security risk — is enormous.

### Why BPO Companies Need Workforce Intelligence

A BPO operation may have 500+ employees, multiple shifts, remote employees, hybrid teams, multiple client accounts, and a stack of CRM platforms, ticketing systems, email systems, cloud applications, customer databases and third-party tools.

Traditional attendance systems can tell management: *Employee logged in → Employee logged out.* But that doesn't answer the more important questions — was the employee productive? Which applications were being used? Was customer information accessed appropriately? Was time being lost on non-business activities? Were files being transferred to unauthorized locations? Are certain teams experiencing productivity problems?

This is the gap between attendance monitoring and workforce intelligence.

## 🔐 Human Activity Is Also a Cybersecurity Concern

The latest Verizon 2026 DBIR shows that the human element remains a major component of cyber risk across industries. For example, in the Financial & Insurance sector, the human element was involved in 65% of breaches.

BPO organizations face a similar fundamental challenge: employees have access to customer data. That means cybersecurity isn't only about firewall, antivirus, EDR and SIEM — it is also about people, behavior, applications, data and context. A BPO company therefore needs visibility into what is happening at the endpoint and workforce level.

---

## 🚨 The Hidden Productivity Problem

Imagine a BPO employee working an 8-hour shift, logged into the corporate system the entire time. But during those eight hours, the employee may also spend time on social media, personal email, shopping websites, streaming platforms, unproductive browsing, personal applications, or excessive idle time.

Even a relatively small amount of lost time can become significant when multiplied across hundreds or thousands of employees:

> 1,000 employees × 30 minutes/day = 500 hours of potential productive time every working day, or approximately 130,000 hours per year at 260 working days.

The objective is not to assume that all this time is lost — it's to measure where working time is actually going and identify opportunities for improvement.
