# VisuN AI for Banking & Finance: Workforce Intelligence for Productivity, Accountability & Security

Banking and financial services are undergoing a massive digital transformation. Customers expect instant digital services, employees work across multiple applications and cloud platforms, and financial institutions increasingly rely on AI, automation and remote operations.

But this transformation creates a critical challenge: **how can banks and financial institutions ensure that their workforce is productive, accountable and handling sensitive financial information securely?**

For banking and finance, workforce intelligence is no longer just an HR requirement. It is becoming part of operational resilience and cybersecurity.

---

## 📊 The Numbers Banking Leaders Should Not Ignore

IBM's 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Report found that the average cost of a data breach in India reached ₹25.5 crore, an increase of 15.9% from the previous year.

Even more significant for this industry: **financial services recorded the highest average breach cost in India at ₹40.9 crore.** IBM also reported that 26% of malicious breaches in India were AI-generated, demonstrating how rapidly the threat landscape is changing. (IBM India News Room)

For banks and financial institutions, a security incident can affect much more than IT infrastructure. It can impact: customer trust, financial loss, regulatory compliance, business continuity and reputation.

---

## 🏦 Banking Is Becoming More Digital — and More Complex

Modern financial organizations operate across core banking systems, internet banking, mobile banking, payment platforms, CRM systems, loan-processing applications, KYC/AML systems, trading platforms, financial applications, cloud services, email, customer databases and AI applications.

An employee may interact with dozens of systems every day. Traditional attendance systems can tell management: *Login 9:00 AM, Logout 6:00 PM.* But banking leaders need deeper answers: **what happened during those nine hours?**

## 🔐 The Human Element Matters

The Reserve Bank of India continues to identify cybersecurity, fraud detection and operational resilience as important priorities for the financial system. Its 2025 Annual Report notes that cyber risk remains a major operational risk and highlights initiatives around cyber-incident monitoring, IT governance, third-party IT service providers, digital forensic readiness and cyber resilience. (Reserve Bank of India)

RBI data also shows the scale and complexity of banking fraud. During 2024-25, banks reported 23,953 fraud cases involving ₹36,014 crore in reported amounts. Importantly, RBI notes that the amount involved does not necessarily equal the actual loss, since recoveries and other factors can reduce the loss incurred. (Reserve Bank of India)

This demonstrates why financial institutions need to look beyond traditional perimeter security.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Workforce Activity Is Part of the Security Equation

Consider a banking employee working in loan processing. A normal workflow could be:

> Employee → Loan Application → Customer Record → Approved Document Repository

But imagine another pattern:

> Employee → Customer Database → Large Download → Personal Cloud → External Application

The second pattern may require investigation. The challenge is not necessarily to assume that the employee has done something wrong — it is to have sufficient visibility to identify unusual behavior and investigate it appropriately.

## 🚀 How VisuN AI Can Help Banking & Finance

**1. Workforce Productivity Intelligence** — Banking operations involve large teams performing highly structured processes. VisuN AI can provide insight into application usage, website activity, active/idle patterns, employee activity, work patterns, productivity trends, team-level performance indicators and management dashboards. This helps organizations move from *"Employees are logged in"* to *"We understand how working time is being utilized."*

**2. Protect Sensitive Financial Information** — Financial organizations handle customer identity information, account information, loan records, credit information, financial statements, transaction information, KYC documents, investment information, payment information and internal financial data. VisuN AI can provide additional visibility around files, applications, websites, devices and user activity, complementing existing security technologies such as DLP, EDR/XDR, SIEM, IAM, SOC and CASB/SASE.

**3. Strengthen Insider-Risk Visibility** — Insider risk can arise from compromised employee accounts, excessive access, unauthorized downloads, unusual file activity, personal cloud usage, USB transfers, unauthorized applications or improper handling of customer information. For example:

> Normal: Customer Service → CRM → Ticketing → Approved Knowledge Base
> Unusual: Customer Service → Customer Database → Bulk Download → External Cloud

**4. Monitor Shadow AI Risk** — Employees may use AI for document summarization, customer-service assistance, research, report preparation, coding, data analysis and productivity automation. But unauthorized AI usage can create data-governance risks if customer information, financial data or confidential business information is entered into an unauthorized AI service. VisuN AI can provide visibility into application and URL activity, helping organizations establish appropriate AI-use policies.

**5. Improve Remote & Hybrid Workforce Visibility** — Financial institutions increasingly operate distributed teams: remote employees, hybrid employees, branch employees, back-office teams, third-party service providers and outsourced operations. VisuN AI can provide centralized workforce intelligence across distributed environments, helping answer who is active, which applications are being used, and where productivity gaps are occurring.

**6. Support Regulatory & Audit Readiness** — RBI's recent supervisory focus includes cybersecurity, IT governance, third-party IT risk, digital forensic readiness, fraud risk management, early-warning mechanisms and cyber-incident monitoring. (Reserve Bank of India) VisuN AI is not a regulatory certification or replacement for required controls, but workforce activity intelligence can complement broader governance, security and investigation processes.

**7. Improve Operational Efficiency** — Banking organizations compete on speed, accuracy, customer experience, cost and security. Workforce intelligence can help identify excessive idle time, application bottlenecks, process inefficiencies, underutilized resources and repetitive activities — not to increase workload, but to determine how existing workforce capacity can be used more effectively.

## 📈 The Business Case

Consider a financial organization with 1,000 employees. If workforce intelligence helps identify just 30 minutes of potentially recoverable productive capacity per employee per day, that represents **500 hours per day**, or approximately **130,000 hours per year** (assuming 260 working days).

The actual financial benefit depends on employee cost, role, process and utilization. But the calculation demonstrates an important point: at banking scale, even small improvements in workforce productivity can create significant business value.
