# VisuN AI for Insurance: Turning Workforce Visibility into Business Protection

The insurance industry runs on trust, sensitive information, accuracy, and speed. Every day, insurance companies handle customer identities, policy information, medical records, financial documents, claims data, payment information and confidential business records — and organizations are becoming increasingly digital, distributed and dependent on employees, third-party vendors and cloud applications.

This creates an important question: **how can an insurance company know that its people, data and digital resources are being used productively, securely and responsibly?** This is where VisuN AI can become an important part of the modern insurance technology and security strategy.

---

## Why Insurance Companies Need Workforce Intelligence

Insurance companies typically have multiple teams working simultaneously — claims processing, underwriting, policy administration, customer service, finance and accounting, sales and distribution, brokers and agents, IT and support, back-office operations, remote and hybrid employees, and third-party service providers.

Traditional attendance systems can tell management *"is the employee logged in?"* but they cannot answer *"what is happening during those working hours?"* VisuN AI is designed to provide a deeper layer of workforce visibility, productivity intelligence and accountability.

## The Numbers Insurance Leaders Should Pay Attention To

**1. Financial and insurance organizations remain a major cyber target.** According to Verizon's 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report, the financial and insurance sector recorded 3,809 security incidents, including 1,300 confirmed data-disclosure breaches. System Intrusion, Social Engineering and "Everything Else" accounted for 81% of breaches in the sector, and the human element was involved in 65% of breaches. Cybersecurity cannot stop at firewalls, endpoint protection and network monitoring — organizations also need visibility into human activity and employee behavior.

**2. A data breach can cost Indian organizations ₹25.5 crore.** IBM's 2026 Cost of a Data Breach research reports that the average cost of a data breach in India reached approximately ₹25.5 crore, with the financial services sector recording an even higher average of approximately ₹40.9 crore. A single compromised account or inappropriate data transfer can create: data exposure → investigation → business disruption → regulatory consequences → financial loss → reputation damage.

**3. AI is changing the threat landscape.** IBM's 2026 research found that 26% of malicious breaches in India were AI-generated. Globally, one in four malicious breaches studied were AI-enabled, and AI-enabled breaches cost approximately $6 million on average. Insurance organizations are adopting AI for claims processing, fraud detection, customer service, underwriting, document processing, risk assessment and analytics — but employees may use unauthorized AI tools, upload confidential documents or copy customer information without adequate organizational controls. This is commonly referred to as **Shadow AI.**

## The Hidden Risk: Employee Activity

Consider an insurance employee working on a customer claim. During the day, the employee may open the customer policy record, download a document, open email, upload a file to a cloud service, use a personal messaging platform, visit an external website, copy information into another application, access an AI tool, or send information to another person.

Traditional attendance software may simply record *login/logout* times. But management may actually need to understand what applications were used, what websites were accessed, how much time was spent on productive applications, whether sensitive files were accessed, and whether unusual activities were detected. This is the gap that workforce intelligence attempts to address.

## How VisuN AI Can Help Insurance Companies

**1. Employee Productivity Intelligence** — Instead of measuring productivity purely through attendance, organizations can analyze active working time, application usage, website activity, work patterns, idle time, task activity, employee utilization and team-level productivity trends.

**2. Data Protection and Insider Risk Visibility** — VisuN AI can complement existing cybersecurity controls with visibility into files, applications, websites, email, cloud platforms, USB devices and data movement. For example:

> Normal: Claims employee → Claims application → Approved document repository
> Potentially unusual: Claims employee → Customer database → Large file download → Personal cloud service

**3. Reduce Productivity Leakage** — Even without intentional wrongdoing, organizations can lose significant productive time through social media, excessive browsing, streaming, personal applications, unproductive websites, excessive idle time and unnecessary application switching. The goal should be **Measure → Understand → Improve**, rather than *Monitor → Punish.*

**4. Remote and Hybrid Workforce Visibility** — Insurance organizations increasingly operate across corporate offices, branch offices, remote and hybrid employees, outsourced operations, BPO/KPO partners and third-party service providers. VisuN AI can provide centralized workforce visibility while allowing organizations to establish appropriate access, privacy and monitoring policies.

**5. Protect Customer Information** — Depending on the business line, employees may handle customer identity information, policy information, claims information, financial information, health-related information, documents, contact information and payment information. VisuN AI can complement DLP, EDR, SIEM and IAM solutions by adding another layer of workforce and endpoint intelligence.

**6. Improve Accountability** — When something goes wrong, management may need to understand who accessed the information, which application was used, when the activity occurred, and whether it was normal or unusual. A strong activity trail can help improve accountability, internal investigations, operational governance, incident response and process improvement.

**7. Help Reduce Human-Related Cyber Risk** — The 2026 Verizon DBIR reports that the human element was involved in 65% of financial and insurance breaches. Cybersecurity teams traditionally focus on network, endpoint, identity and application — but modern organizations also need people, behavior and context. VisuN AI can contribute to that additional visibility layer.
