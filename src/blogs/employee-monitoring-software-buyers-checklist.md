# Employee Monitoring Software — A Buyer's Checklist for Startups and SMBs

Enterprise monitoring platforms are often built for security teams with dedicated headcount to manage them — overkill, and overpriced, for a startup or small business trying to get visibility into a lean, often remote, team.

Here's what actually matters when you're choosing a tool without an enterprise budget.

---

## 1. Pricing That Scales With Headcount, Not Against It

Many platforms price by feature tier or require annual contracts that don't make sense for a 10–50 person team. Look for:

- Transparent per-endpoint pricing
- No forced long-term lock-in to get started
- Room to grow without a painful re-negotiation

VisuN AI starts at **$2 per endpoint per month**, which keeps monitoring accessible instead of something you delay "until we're bigger."

---

## 2. Setup That Doesn't Need a Dedicated IT Team

If a tool needs a week of configuration and a specialist to maintain it, it's the wrong fit for most SMBs. Prioritise:

- A lightweight agent with minimal CPU impact
- Simple, clear dashboards any manager can use without technical training
- Fast rollout across existing devices

## 4. Legal and Ethical Fit

A tool is only as good as how responsibly it's deployed. Before buying, confirm the platform supports:

- Transparent disclosure to employees
- A clear, written monitoring policy
- Data collection limited to what's actually needed
- Compliance alignment if you're pursuing frameworks like ISO 27001 or SOC 2 — see our [compliance monitoring guide](https://www.globalcyberassociates.com/blog/iso-27001-soc2-compliance-monitoring)

If a vendor can't clearly explain what is and isn't collected, that's a red flag regardless of price.
