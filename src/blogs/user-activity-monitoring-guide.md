# User Activity Monitoring (UAM): The Complete Guide

You can't secure what you can't see. **User Activity Monitoring (UAM)** gives organisations a clear, real-time picture of what's happening on their endpoints and networks — who is doing what, where, and when.

It's the foundation of insider risk programs, compliance audits, and modern security operations.

---

## What Is User Activity Monitoring?

User Activity Monitoring is the practice of **recording and analysing how users interact with company systems**. Done well, it produces an accurate, time-stamped record of activity that security and IT teams can use to detect risk, investigate incidents, and prove compliance.

Typical signals include:

- Applications and websites used
- File operations (create, open, copy, move, delete)
- USB and removable-device connections
- Logins, privilege changes, and access to sensitive systems
- Network connections and device inventory

---

## Why UAM Matters

### 🛡️ Detect Insider Risk
Most damaging incidents involve *authorised* users. UAM surfaces the unusual behaviour that perimeter tools miss. (Deep-dive: [insider threat detection](https://www.globalcyberassociates.com/blog/insider-threat-detection-guide).)

### 🔎 Faster Incident Investigation
When something goes wrong, a complete activity timeline turns days of guesswork into minutes of evidence.

### 📋 Compliance & Audit
Standards like ISO 27001 and SOC 2 expect you to track access to sensitive data and retain audit trails. ([ISO 27001 & SOC 2 monitoring](https://www.globalcyberassociates.com/blog/iso-27001-soc2-compliance-monitoring).)

### 📈 Operational Insight
Aggregate trends reveal bottlenecks, shadow IT, and process gaps — useful well beyond security.

## How User Activity Monitoring Works

1. **A lightweight agent** runs on each endpoint, capturing activity with minimal performance impact.
2. **Events are streamed** to a central dashboard in real time.
3. **Baselines are established** for what's normal per user and per role.
4. **Anomalies trigger alerts** with full context — who, what, when, where.
5. **Audit trails are retained** for investigation and compliance.

---

## UAM Done Responsibly

Monitoring must respect employees:

- ✅ Be **transparent** — publish a clear monitoring policy.
- ✅ Collect **only what's needed** for security and compliance.
- ✅ Use **role-based access** so only authorised staff see sensitive data.
- ✅ **Log, don't silently block** — keep evidence and context.
- ✅ Comply with local **data-protection laws**.
